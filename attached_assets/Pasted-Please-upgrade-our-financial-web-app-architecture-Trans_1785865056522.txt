### Key Features & Requirements:

1. Project-Based P&L Module (Multi-Business Accounting):
   - Ability to create dynamically separate Projects/Businesses (e.g. "TikTok Monetization", "YouTube Channel", "Cleaning Company", "Luna Garden", etc.).
   - For each project, store and calculate:
     - Gross Revenue (Оборот)
     - Cost of Goods/Services Sold / Direct Expenses (Закуп / Прямые расходы)
     - Gross Profit / Margin (Маржа / Валовая прибыль)
     - Operating Expenses (Маркетинг, Зарплата, Аренда, Логистика, Коммуналка)
     - Net Profit (Чистая прибыль)
     - Reinvestment Amount (Реинвестиции)
     - Dividends Paid out to Personal Account (Дивиденды)
   - Summary view comparing performance across all projects (Bar charts for revenue/profit per project).

2. Davlatov's Wealth Accumulation & Allocation System (Personal Funds Engine):
   - When Dividends or Personal Income is recorded, apply the automatic distribution algorithm (based on Saidmurod Davlatov's methodology):
     a) Charity (Благотворительность): 2.5% - 10%
     b) Parents Fund (Родителям): 10%
     c) Future / Savings Fund (На будущее / Накопления): 10%
     d) Entertainment Fund (Развлечения): 10%
     e) Living & Business Growth (Собственный счет & Капитал): Remaining budget divided into Growth vs Dream funds (50% Large Dreams, 50% Small Dreams).

3. Savings & Financial Target Planner (Goals & Milestones):
   - Interactive Target Setting:
     - User inputs target savings goals: 1 Month, 6 Months, 1 Year, 3 Years.
     - System calculates required monthly target net income to hit these goals.
     - Progress bar tracking status towards each savings milestone.

4. UI/UX Dashboard Enhancements (Inspired by Looker/Data Studio Dashboards):
   - Top Metrics Bar: Total Gross Revenue, Total Net Profit, Total Dividends, Total Debt / Credit, Total Emergency Savings.
   - Monthly Breakdown Visualizer: Clean stacked bar/line charts showing Revenue vs Expenses vs Reinvestments vs Dividends per month.
   - All amounts rendered in local currency 'сом' (KGS).
